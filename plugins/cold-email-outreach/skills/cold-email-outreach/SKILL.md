# Cold Email and Outreach

## Inputs
Ideal customer, the offer, one piece of real proof, and the single action wanted.

## Sequence (4 touches over 10-14 days)
1. Short first email: first line from a real observation about the prospect, one problem, one proof point, one ask.
2. Follow-up adding new value (a result, a resource), not just checking in.
3. Different angle.
4. Polite close.

## Style
- Plain text, under 90 words, subject under 5 words.
- No images, no attachments, at most one link (none in the first email if possible).
- Never fabricate claims or results.

## Deliverability checklist
- SPF, DKIM and DMARC set up.
- Send from a separate domain, not the main one.
- Warm up new inboxes and stay under about 50 emails a day per inbox.

## Compliance
Include a clear way to opt out. For EU prospects, check GDPR (legitimate interest for B2B); for the US, CAN-SPAM.

Also write a LinkedIn version: a 300-character connection note and one follow-up.
